Northstar Commerce Account Recovery
Effective date: 2026-01-01. Owner: Identity. Related: billing.txt.

Password reset is self-service. From the login page, choose Forgot password and enter the email address on the account. Northstar sends a one-time link that expires after 30 minutes and can be used only once. Links are not extended; request a new one if it expires. The message comes from accounts@northstar.example and will land in spam for some corporate filters — ask the customer to search for "Northstar" before assuming it was never sent.

No access to the email. If the customer no longer controls that inbox, Identity will not reset the password from a chat transcript alone. The customer must file a ticket with: (1) the email currently on the account, (2) the order number of a purchase in the last 90 days, and (3) the last four digits of the card used on that order or the gift-card code. Identity replies within one business day with a replacement-email form. Social-login accounts (Google, Apple) must recover through that provider; Northstar cannot reset those credentials.

Two-factor authentication. Admin and Finance roles require TOTP two-factor. Losing a TOTP device is recovered with a backup code generated at enrollment. If backup codes are also lost, a director in the customer's organization must email security@northstar.example from the domain on the contract. Support agents must not disable two-factor from the ticket tool.

Lockouts. Five failed password attempts in 15 minutes lock the account for 30 minutes. A lockout is not a password reset. Telling a locked-out customer to "just reset the password" works, but they can also wait out the 30 minutes. Credential-stuffing events (hundreds of failures from many IPs) automatically force a reset and email the customer; those are visible in the Identity dashboard as force_reset events.

Session hygiene. Password reset invalidates all existing sessions on the account, including mobile. Warn customers they will be signed out of the iOS and Android apps. API tokens are not revoked by a password reset; developers must rotate keys separately (see rate_limits.txt).

Phishing. Northstar will never ask for a password, TOTP code, or backup code in email or chat. Agents who see a customer paste a password into the ticket must rotate it immediately and file a Sev-2 with Security.
